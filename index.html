The basics

Colour theory,
made simple.

Colour isn't picked at random. Pick two that sit close for harmony, two from opposite sides for contrast, or one to lead them all. These three moves cover almost every scheme we build.

60%30%10%
The 60-30-10 rule

Iconic Family

Neighbouring hues that share an underlying tone and never fight for attention: 60% dominant on the walls, 30% secondary on furniture or a feature wall, 10% accent on trim and decor. The safest route to a room that feels calm and put together.

Blues → GreensCreams → YellowsNaturals → Metalics
70%30%
The 70-30 rule

Dynamic Duo

Two colours from opposite sides of the wheel that make each other look more alive. Let one lead at around 70% with the other on an accent wall, door or trim. Push to 80-20 for bolder contrast. Avoid 50/50: it reads as indecisive.

Blues + YellowsGreens + PinksNaturals + Blues
90–100%10%
The 90-100% rule

Hero

One shade covers walls, trim and often furnishings, with white, black or grey only for breathing room. The boldest of the three and the strongest statement, best for a single standout room.

Our story

Transforming spaces through the art of colour.

Hermosa Paints is a vibrant, innovative company established with a vision to provide high-quality paints that inspire creativity and enhance aesthetics. We've grown to become one of Kenya's most trusted paint brands.

From interior walls to busy highways, from wooden furniture to metal structures, our comprehensive range of coatings is engineered for Kenya's climate, tested for durability and crafted for beauty.

Vision

To be East Africa's most trusted and innovative paint company, delivering transformative colour solutions that elevate every space, inspire every builder, and brighten every life.

Mission

Manufacturing paints and building solutions that make your projects beautiful, durable and sustainable, backed by expert guidance, accessible pricing and a passion for colour.
